## 🚀 What's New in v1.0.2

### ✨ Highlights & New Features
- **🔁 Loop Video SidePanel Toggle**: Added a dedicated **"Loop Video"** toggle switch in the sidepanel menu to easily toggle between endless looping and single play modes.
- **🙈 Long-Press Card Hiding**: Long-press any video card in the browse feed to instantly hide it, accompanied by tactile haptic feedback and a smooth glassmorphic **"Card Hidden"** overlay badge.
- **👆 Two-Finger Swipe Down Refresh**: Added a multi-finger gesture to the video player—swipe down with two fingers anywhere on the video to refresh your feed.
- **📊 Centered Gesture HUD Indicators**: Repositioned volume and brightness gesture indicators to a sleek, symmetrical top-center glassmorphic pill bar.
- **🎨 Obsidian Glassmorphism & Sleek Dark Surfaces**: Darkened surface colors to deep obsidian (`#101218`) and fixed corner rounding across sidepanel cards (`14.dp` radius).

### 🛠️ UX & Player Improvements
- **⏩ Double-Tap Seeking**: Double-tap left/right on video edges to seek ±10 seconds with animated seek feedback popups ("« 10s" / "10s »").
- **🔍 Debounced Search & Recent History**: Added 300ms query debounce and persistent recent search history chips in Search.
- **📳 Tactile Haptic Feedback**: Integrated subtle haptics across all gestures, seeks, likes, subscribe toggles, and card actions.
- **🎛️ Redesigned SidePanel Menu**: Rebuilt sidepanel navigation with expandable sort selections, smooth collapse animations, and glass card styling.
- **💀 Shimmer Loading & Empty Feed UI**: Added pulsing skeleton cards during initial load and a dedicated empty feed screen with a one-tap refresh button.
- **🔗 Native Video Link Sharing**: Added a Share button in the player action row to send Reddit post permalinks.
- **🧹 Cache Clear Confirmation**: Added safety confirmation dialog before clearing thumbnail cache in About.
